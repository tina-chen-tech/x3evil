X-3VIL is a Tokyo-born, globally-influenced tattoo artist who bridges the gap between classic irezumi and modern anime aesthetics. Each design begins with a conversation: your vision, your favorite series, the characters and worlds that shaped you. It evolves into a one-of-a-kind piece that moves with your body.

Trained in traditional Japanese tebori and fluent in digital illustration, X-3VIL brings a dual discipline to every tattoo: the precision of a fine artist and the fluid energy of anime storytelling.

About
Ink flows from two hands: one holds the machine, the other holds the brush.
X-3VIL is an artist based in Hangzhou... Between sessions, he draws. Scrolls, mostly: hand-drawn anime character scrolls that unroll across meters of paper.
Influenced by Japanese irezumi, cyberpunk, and the endless character designs that come out of the anime industry.
